Top Luxury Destinations

North Coast

Nungwi & Kendwa

Famous for non-tidal, crystal-clear turquoise waters available for swimming all day long.

  • Zuri Zanzibar Member of Design Hotels, private villas, spice gardens.
  • The Riu Palace Zanzibar All-inclusive luxury featuring oceanfront suites & fine dining.
East Coast

Matemwe & Michamvi

Ideal for ultimate seclusion, low-density luxury, and untouched coral reefs.

  • &Beyond Mnemba Island Ultra-exclusive private island sanctuary with 12 luxury suites.
  • The Palms & Baraza Resort Swahili-Arabic grandeur, private plunge pools & personal butler service.
Pemba Island

Off-Grid Sanctuary

A pristine untouched island surrounded by deep marine channels and lush rainforests.

  • The Manta Resort Home to the world-famous underwater bedroom suite.

Signature Luxury Experiences

Private Dhow Cruises

Charter a traditional luxury wooden dhow for sunset champagne sails around Kendwa or Menai Bay.

Mnemba Atoll Diving

Private yacht charters to rich coral gardens with wild dolphins and marine turtles.

The Rock Restaurant

Iconic dining experience built on a natural coral rock formation in the Indian Ocean.

Best Time to Visit

Season Months Highlights
Dry Season June to October Clear blue skies, comfortable temperatures (25°C–28°C), and perfect beach weather.
Hot Dry Season December to February Warm tropical ocean temperatures, best for scuba diving and marine activities.
