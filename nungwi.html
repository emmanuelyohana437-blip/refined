Why Northern Zanzibar Has No Tide Restrictions

A sharp continental underwater drop-off keeps turquoise ocean water at your feet around the clock.

Nungwi & Kendwa (North)

Deep Coastal Drop-Off

Steep bathymetric slope ensures ocean depth remains right at the sandy shoreline all day long.

  • Swimmable 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Soft, sea-urchin-free sandy seabed
  • Perfect for kayaks & stand-up paddleboarding
Paje & Jozani Coast (East)

Shallow Tidal Reef Flat

Broad shallow reef flats cause the ocean water to retreat up to 1,500 meters twice daily.

  • Ocean recedes up to 1.5 km at low tide
  • Swimming restricted to short high-tide windows
  • Exposed coral flats & sharp sea urchins

Travel Context

Practical Tips & Information

Key information for planning your day trip to Zanzibar's north coast.

Jozani Chwaka Bay National Park is Zanzibar's premier terrestrial national park, famous for red colobus monkeys and mangrove swamps in the central island. In contrast, Nungwi and Kendwa represent Northern Zanzibar's marine highlight, offering non-stop deep ocean swimming unaffected by low tides.
The drive from Stone Town or Zanzibar International Airport to Nungwi/Kendwa takes approximately 60 to 75 minutes (around 60 km) along smooth paved roads. Private taxis and transfers operate 24/7.
The dry seasons from June to October and December to March offer calm water, clear underwater visibility (up to 30 meters), and minimal wind, making conditions perfect for ocean swimming and dhow sailing.
