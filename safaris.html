Game Drives

Game Drives offer an extraordinary opportunity to travel deep into wildlife reserves in customized open-top 4x4 vehicles. You will get up-close views of the Big Five (Lions, Elephants, Leopards, Rhinos, and Buffalos) guided by highly experienced local trackers and naturalists.

Walking Safaris

Experience the raw essence of the African wilderness on foot. Walking safaris allow you to observe animal tracks, indigenous medicinal plants, and insect life close up. Accompanied by armed wildlife rangers and experienced guides, safety and immersion are guaranteed.

Bird Watching

Explore over 1,100 species of rare and exotic birds residing across diverse wetlands, riverine forests, and open savannahs. Perfect for photography and birding enthusiasts wanting to witness migratory species alongside native African birds.
