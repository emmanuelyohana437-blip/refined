Matemwe Coast (North-East)

Outer Coral Barrier & Reef Lagoon

An offshore barrier reef breaks ocean swells, creating a calm, turquoise lagoon ideal for high-tide swimming and Mnemba boat launches.

  • High-tide swimming in warm turquoise lagoon
  • Direct gateway to Mnemba Atoll marine reserve

Michamvi Kae & Pingwe (East)

Sheltered Bay & Mangrove Haven

Nestled on Chwaka Bay, Michamvi Kae faces west over calm waters, offering rare East Coast sunsets and tranquil mangrove channels.

  • Sheltered water ideal for paddleboarding & kayaking
  • Unique East Coast west-facing sunset sandbar
  • Lush mangrove forests teeming with birdlife

Mnemba Atoll Marine Sanctuary

Located directly off Matemwe Beach, Mnemba Atoll is an oval conservation ring protected by local marine rangers. Home to bottlenose dolphins, green sea turtles, clownfish, and over 600 species of tropical fish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential information for planning your Matemwe & Michamvi escape.

The ocean retreats significantly twice daily along Zanzibar's East Coast. High tide is ideal for swimming in Matemwe's turquoise lagoon and launching boat trips to Mnemba Atoll. Low tide is ideal for guided reef walks and exploring Michamvi Kae's shallow sandbars.
Driving to Matemwe takes approximately 55-60 minutes (45 km) from Stone Town or the airport. Transit to Michamvi takes roughly 65-75 minutes (62 km). Taxis and private transfers run smoothly along paved arterial roads.
Always wear mineral reef-safe sunscreen to protect delicate coral reefs. Do not touch or step on living corals or starfish. When passing through local fishing villages in Matemwe or Michamvi, please dress modestly out of respect for Zanzibari Swahili culture.
