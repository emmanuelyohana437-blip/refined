Why Visit Serengeti?

The Serengeti offers an unmatched wildlife experience. From the dramatic river crossings of the migration to the quiet moments watching a lion pride rest under an acacia tree, every day brings something new. It is also one of the best places in Africa to see the Big Five in a single safari.

Travel Time to Serengeti

  • Arusha → Serengeti (by road) 6–7 hours
  • Arusha → Serengeti (by flight) 1 hour
  • Ngorongoro → Serengeti 2–3 hours
  • Tarangire → Serengeti 4–5 hours
